# Job Opportunity Profile (example)

This file is read by the job-opportunities scheduled agent (see
`docs/production-controls-runbook.md`) to rank scraped listings for fit and
personal interest. Replace it with your own profile, or point
`DM_JOB_OPPORTUNITIES_PROFILE_PATH` at a private copy — see
`docs/production-controls-runbook.md` for the full env var list.

## Identity And Contact

- Full name
- Location
- Email
- Portfolio / personal site
- GitHub
- LinkedIn

## Education

- Degrees, institutions, years

## Core Positioning

A short paragraph describing your background and the kind of role where you
are strongest: domains, technical strengths, and what differentiates you.

List good-fit categories, e.g.:

- Domain A where your background gives you an edge.
- Domain B you're interested in but only when it uses skill X.
- Role types (staff/senior/principal, research, engineering, etc.).

## Skills

- Group skills by category (languages, frameworks, domain tools, etc.).

## Preferences

- Compensation, remote/hybrid/onsite, location constraints.
- Company size, stage, or mission preferences.

## Hard Exclusions

- Anything that should always be filtered out (e.g. unpaid roles,
  internships, roles requiring credentials you don't hold).
